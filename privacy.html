Privacy Notice

Pre-launch online profile · Updated November 2026

This notice describes the information requested when you create an online access profile on the Vercel Bank website.

Information collected

The registration form requests your name, email address, phone number, country of residence, and a password. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. Passwords are processed by the authentication provider and are not stored in the website's public profile metadata.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure your online profile, verify your email address, support login and account recovery, and communicate about the profile.

Financial information

Do not submit bank account numbers, card numbers, PINs, government identity numbers, or financial credentials through this pre-launch registration form. This form does not perform customer due diligence or open a financial account.

Service providers and security

Supabase provides authentication infrastructure. Access controls and security settings should be reviewed before public launch. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may choose not to create a profile. Requests to access, correct, or delete profile information should be made through the published support contact, subject to applicable law.

Updates and contact

This notice must be reviewed and completed with the operator's legal identity, contact details, retention periods, lawful bases, and applicable privacy rights before public launch.
